### Battery / nightstand overlap a table box and the hut facade  ×4

In the hut, the 2.4 m-wide table’s bounding box (x 7.30–9.70) overlaps the nightstand at (7,53) where the battery lies, and the facade walls touch the nightstand. The game builds the same boxes: its prop colliders are bounding boxes of the models, which are solid in the box but open underneath in reality.

- Evidence: [running game] prop colliders in the hut: table x 7.3–9.7, z 52.93–54.07; nightstand x 7.24–7.76 (same as the import)
- Evidence: saint-marrow/js/world/level.js placeProp(): col = propCollider(obj, 0.0) — the model’s bounding box

### Hut interior behind a 0.43 m gap  ×3

Right behind the hut door the table and the tool chest leave a 0.43 m gap, narrower than the player (0.6 m). The Studio’s navigation, and a strict collision flood-fill of the original, cannot get through, so items deeper in the hut count as unreachable. The original’s push-out collision may let a player squeeze through — the Studio does not claim to know. The boltcutters and crowbar can be picked up from the doorway and are not flagged.

- Evidence: [running game] hut prop colliders equal the import to < 1 cm; the game’s own findInteractable() cannot pick these items from any position a radius-0.3 circle can reach (studio/tests/browser/original-oracle.test.mjs)
- Evidence: Studio and original agree on geometry, so this is about player squeeze tolerance, not import accuracy

- `interaction-out-of-reach` Interaction volume "use:fusebox" is centred at y=2.9 m: a standing player (eye 1.62 m) must look 54.888° up from 0.9 m away.
